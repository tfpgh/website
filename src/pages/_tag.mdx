I forced two robots to play tag against each other for five (simulated) years
before releasing them into the real world. Why? It sounded really cool to watch!

This writeup is an overview of everything required to make tag happen and
the results I observed. I also hope to motivate each part without devolving into
the scary looking jargon of
[reinforcement learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinforcement_learning).

### Hardware
First, we need some **robots**. I looked for a little while and couldn't find
any existing kits or designs that were affordable, fast, easily programmable,
and didn't have loads of extra sensors. So I built my own.
